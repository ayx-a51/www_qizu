Account & Data Deletion

Fastest way: in the app
Open QIZU → Settings → Delete account. After a confirmation and a short re-sign-in, your account and all your data (sets, cards, statistics) are permanently deleted from our servers, immediately and on all your devices.

Alternatively: by email

  1. Write to ayx@a51.io from the email address linked to your account.
  2. Use the subject line: DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. By default, all accounts associated with your email address for apps published by a51.io will be deleted — mention QIZU in the message if you only want this app's data removed.

What happens next

  • Email requests are processed within up to 5 business days.
  • Deletion is permanent — sets, cards and statistics cannot be recovered.
  • Residual copies in operational backups are purged within up to 365 days.
  • An active QIZU+ subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account — manage it in Google Play subscriptions.
