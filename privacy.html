Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

QIZU holds the quiz sets you build and how you are doing with them. This page describes exactly what is stored, where, and how to remove it.

Data we collect

You can use QIZU without an account — the app then stores your data under a random anonymous identifier, and we receive no personal information about you. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, display name and profile picture. When you sign in with Apple we receive your email address and, if you choose to share it, your name; if you choose Apple's Hide My Email, we receive only a private relay address from Apple that forwards to your inbox, never your real address. Either way, this is used solely to identify your account. In every case the app stores the quiz sets and cards you create and your practice statistics (cards answered, time practiced, streaks). If you subscribe to QIZU+, we also keep a record of that subscription (see Subscriptions below). We do not collect any other personal data.

Purpose of data collection

Your data is used solely to sync your quiz library across your devices, to show you your own learning statistics within the app, and to unlock QIZU+ on your devices when you subscribe or redeem a code.

Data storage and security

We store your data securely on Google's Firebase platform (Cloud Firestore, hosted in Zürich, Switzerland), encrypted in transit. Crash reports — device model, operating system, app version and crash traces, never your quiz content — are processed by Firebase Crashlytics to keep the app reliable, grouped under a random crash-reporting installation identifier that is not linked to your account, and can be turned off at any time under Settings → Privacy. Firebase App Check verifies that requests come from the genuine app, using a Play Integrity attestation on Android or Apple's App Attest (DeviceCheck on older devices) on iPhone, and a Firebase installation ID (not tied to you or your device hardware). A random per-installation code is stored with the account to make the free word-list import retry-safe, and redeeming a QIZU+ code stores the code and the time of redemption with the account. As with any Firebase-backed app, Google records the IP address and user agent of requests for sign-in security and in function logs.

Two kinds of content are fetched from static web servers rather than Firebase: ready-made word lists from qizu.net (served by GitHub Pages) and, with QIZU+, card audio from audio.qizu.net (served by Cloudflare). Like any web server, they record the IP address and user agent of each request in their logs. A request names only the list or the audio file being fetched — audio files are identified by an opaque code, and your own cards are never sent to either server.

If you report a problem with a card from within the app, the report you choose to send contains that card's text, the answer you typed, the kind of problem and your optional note, and is stored in Firestore with your account so we can fix the content. Reports are deleted together with your account.

Cookies and tracking

QIZU does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics, or any tracking technologies.

Subscriptions

The optional QIZU+ subscription is processed entirely by Google Play on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone. We never see or store your payment details.

On Android, when you subscribe, the app passes Google Play's purchase token for that subscription to our server, which asks Google Play to confirm it and stores, with your account: the token, which plan you chose, the subscription's status (for example active, cancelled or paused), when the current period ends, and when Google last confirmed it.

On iPhone, the app passes the App Store's signed record of your purchase to our server, which checks it with Apple and stores, with your account: the purchase's transaction ID, which plan you chose, the subscription's status, when the current period ends, and when Apple last confirmed it. When you buy while signed in, the app gives Apple an identifier derived from your QIZU account ID, so that Apple's records of the purchase name the account it belongs to, and Apple tells our server about renewals, cancellations and refunds. You can also subscribe on iPhone without signing in: QIZU+ then works on that iPhone only, and nothing about the purchase reaches our server until you sign in, when it is recorded with your account as described above.

This lets QIZU+ follow your account to your other devices, and makes sure one subscription unlocks one account. The server checks again with Google Play or Apple from time to time — for example when a period ends — so that a renewal, a cancellation or a refund reaches all your devices. These records are deleted together with your account; the subscription itself stays with Google Play or the App Store, so cancel it there if you no longer want it.

Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third parties other than Google as part of the Firebase and Google Play services named above, GitHub and Cloudflare, whose servers deliver the word lists and card audio described above, and Apple as part of Sign in with Apple, the App Store and, on iPhone, its App Attest service, which confirms that requests come from the genuine app.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You may delete your account and all data at any time. If you use QIZU without signing in, your data is tied to that device's anonymous identifier; Settings → Delete account is available there too and removes the data and the anonymous identifier itself from our servers (Delete all data removes the data but keeps the identifier). Uninstalling the app without doing so leaves the data stored but permanently inaccessible to anyone, including you.

User groups

All user groups are treated equally. We do not apply different rules or collect different data based on age or other characteristics.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all data directly in the app (Settings → Delete account), or follow our Data Deletion Policy. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also revokes QIZU's Sign in with Apple access to your Apple Account. Individual quiz sets can be exported as CSV inside the app; you may also request a copy of your data via the contact below.

Children

QIZU is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change what QIZU collects, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
